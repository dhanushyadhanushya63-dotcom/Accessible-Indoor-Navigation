Inclusive By Design

Why Traditional Maps Fail Indoors

Most indoor navigation solutions treat indoor spaces as standard 2D floors, completely ignoring step hazards, narrow doors, non-working lifts, and temporary maintenance blockages.

Standard Navigation Pitfalls

Directs wheelchair users into 20-step staircases, routes through wet floors, and fails to announce low doorways or non-tactile surfaces.

Our Obstacle-Aware Route Planner

Pre-screens every meter of corridor, prioritizes ADA-certified ramps and elevators, and provides real-time detours around maintenance barriers.

Accessibility Feature Matrix

Every route generated adheres strictly to universal accessibility guidelines.

  • Ramp gradient verification (never exceeds 1:12 slope)
  • Minimum 36-inch clearance through all mapped doorways
  • Elevator prioritization with vocal announcements
  • Instant high-contrast mode for vision impaired users
