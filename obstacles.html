How Automatic Rerouting Works

When a corridor is marked "Blocked", its corresponding edges in the campus graph are immediately disabled with infinite weight penalty. The Dijkstra algorithm then searches for alternative level paths, ramps, or elevators.

Safety Protocol Standards

Wet floor buffing, paint fume ventilation, and lift maintenance must be reported with minimum 15-minute advance notice to prevent student stranding.
