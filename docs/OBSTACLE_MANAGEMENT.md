# Campus Obstacle Management & Safety Console

Real-time facilities console allowing staff and administrators to report, toggle, and resolve indoor corridor blockages.

## Supported Hazard Categories
- Floor Maintenance & Wet Surface (Buffing/Cleaning)
- Construction & Scaffold Repair
- Broken Wheelchair Lift / Mechanical Outage
- Temporary Equipment Delivery Obstruction
- Hazardous Liquid Spill
